Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 March 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how KOKO collects, uses, stores, and protects information when you use the KOKO mobile app and related website at koko.recipes.

1. Information we collect

  • Account information: your email address and authentication status when you create or link an account.
  • Recipe content: photos, PDFs, URLs, text, and recipe metadata you submit for import, parsing, translation, or storage.
  • Subscription and purchase status: subscription state, entitlement status, and purchase verification data needed to unlock paid features.
  • Usage data: app interactions needed to operate scan credits, restore purchases, prevent abuse, and diagnose failures.
  • Support communications: any information you send to support@koko.recipes.

2. How we use information

  • to create and secure your account
  • to verify your email address
  • to import, organize, and display your recipes
  • to process translations, nutrition estimates, and recipe extraction features
  • to validate subscriptions, purchases, and scan-credit usage
  • to provide support, security monitoring, and service improvements

3. Third-party services

KOKO uses third-party infrastructure providers to operate the service, such as authentication, hosting, storage, payments, and AI-assisted recipe processing. These may include Firebase, Google Cloud services, Apple App Store, Google Play, and AI model providers used to parse or transform recipe content.

4. Recipe uploads and AI processing

When you submit a recipe photo, PDF, link, or extracted text, that content may be processed by cloud services and AI systems for features such as extraction, formatting, translation, and nutrition estimation. Please avoid uploading content you do not have rights to use.

5. Legal bases and retention

We process data to provide the service you request, secure accounts, comply with legal obligations, and maintain our legitimate interests in operating and improving KOKO. We retain data for as long as needed to provide the service, comply with law, resolve disputes, and enforce agreements.

6. Your choices

  • You may stop using the app at any time.
  • You may contact us to request account-related support.
  • You may manage subscription billing through Apple or Google where applicable.

7. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information, but no system can guarantee absolute security.

8. Children

KOKO is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

9. Contact

If you have privacy questions, contact support@koko.recipes.

10. Important note

This document is a practical operating policy for launch and review readiness, not legal advice. You should review and customize it so it precisely matches your live data flows before production launch.
