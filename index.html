Last Updated: April 13, 2026

At KOKO, your privacy is paramount. KOKO is fundamentally designed around saved local content rather than always-online browsing.

1. Local First: The app stores recipes, cookbooks, shopping lists, and meal plans locally using on-device persistence wherever possible.

2. Accounts: You may use KOKO in guest mode or securely sign in (e.g. Sign in with Apple) to sync your recipe library.
